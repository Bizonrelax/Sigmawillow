# Sigmawillow 🌿∑

Welcome to **Sigmawillow**, a personal repository dedicated to my independent mathematical thoughts, ideas, and structural sketches.

## About the Project
This repository serves as a digital notebook for capturing and archiving my own mathematical concepts. The name combines **Sigma** ($\Sigma$), representing structure, summation, and the rigor of exact sciences, with **Willow**, symbolizing natural, organic growth and flexible thinking. 

Inside, you will find:
* Personal mathematical notes and drafts
* Theoretical sketches and formulas
* Independent ideas and explorations

## Why "Sigmawillow"?
Much like a willow tree grows its branches, this space is meant to let thoughts branch out freely, rooted in the foundational soil of mathematics.
